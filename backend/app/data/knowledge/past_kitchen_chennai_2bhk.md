# Modern minimalist kitchen, 2BHK apartment, Chennai (P-1001)
Client brief: replace a dated L-shaped kitchen with a clean, handle-less modern kitchen. Budget INR 7-8 lakh. Floor area 9 sqm.

## Scope delivered
Full demolition of old cabinets and tiles, new acrylic-finish modular cabinets (about 20 sqft of base and wall units per running metre), engineered quartz-look countertop, glazed vitrified backsplash, vitrified floor tiles, premium emulsion paint, sink and tap set, six electrical points and four LED fixtures.

## Outcome
Final cost INR 7.4 lakh including 18 percent GST. Programme: 5 weeks (cabinet fabrication lead time of 4 weeks was the critical path). Client chose to defer a chimney upgrade to stay within budget.

## Lessons
Order cabinets on day one because fabrication is the critical path. Concealed plumbing was replaced after demolition, adding two days.
