# Material guide: tiles and stone
Ceramic tiles are the most economical and suit walls and low-traffic floors. Glazed vitrified tiles are harder and more stain resistant and suit most floors. Large-format porcelain gives fewer grout lines and a premium look but needs a very flat substrate. Granite is durable and economical for countertops; engineered quartz offers consistent colour and low maintenance.
