# Design guidelines: small apartments
Use a restrained palette and repeat materials to make small rooms feel larger. Prefer handle-less or slim-profile cabinets in compact kitchens. Layer lighting: ambient cove lighting, task lighting at work surfaces, accent lighting for features. Light floors enlarge a room; use warm lighting to avoid a clinical feel. Built-in storage beats freestanding furniture in small footprints.
