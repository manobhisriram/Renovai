# Smart-home retrofit, 3BHK, Bengaluru (P-1007)
Client brief: retrofit smart lighting control, a smart lock and four cameras without breaking walls.

## Scope delivered
Zigbee/Matter hub, 18 smart switches, 12 smart downlights, one smart lock and four 2K cameras.

## Outcome
Final cost INR 2.4 lakh including GST. Programme: 2 weeks. Switch retrofit works best when done while electrical points are already open.
