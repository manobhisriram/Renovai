# Historical quote revision: INR 10 lakh kitchen fitted to INR 7 lakh (Q-2041)
The first quote was INR 10.2 lakh (premium cabinets, quartz, glass backsplash).

## How it was fitted to INR 7 lakh
1. Cabinets moved from PU finish to acrylic finish (largest saving, same carcass quality).
2. Countertop moved from premium quartz to granite.
3. Glass backsplash replaced by a ceramic tile splash zone only behind the hob.
4. Designer pendants replaced with standard LED downlights.
The client kept the soft-close hardware and the layout unchanged. Delivered at INR 6.9 lakh.

## Value-engineering guidance
Protect what is hard to change later (waterproofing, plumbing, electrical, cabinet carcass). Save on finishes that can be upgraded later (backsplash, lighting, paint).
