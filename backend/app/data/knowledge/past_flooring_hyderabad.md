# Whole-home flooring and repaint, 3BHK, Hyderabad (P-1005)
Client brief: replace dated flooring across a 95 sqm flat and repaint.

## Scope delivered
Removal of old tiles, 800x800 vitrified tiles throughout, MDF skirting and premium emulsion.

## Outcome
Final cost INR 3.6 lakh including GST. Programme: 4 weeks with the family living elsewhere. Dust control and phased room handover were important.
