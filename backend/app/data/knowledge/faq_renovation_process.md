# Frequently asked questions
## How long does a kitchen renovation take?
Usually four to eight weeks. Cabinet fabrication lead time is typically the critical path, so ordering early matters.

## Can I live in the house during renovation?
Bathrooms and kitchens usually need alternatives for the duration. Flooring and painting can be phased room by room.

## Why is an on-site survey needed?
Photos cannot reveal hidden plumbing, damp or exact dimensions. The survey confirms scope and final price.
