# Premium open kitchen, villa, Bengaluru (P-1002)
Client brief: large open kitchen with island, PU-finish cabinets, premium quartz and designer lighting. Floor area 16 sqm. Budget INR 15 lakh.

## Scope delivered
PU-finish modular units with soft-close hardware, premium quartz countertop and island, glass mosaic backsplash, large-format porcelain floor, designer pendant and profile lighting, concealed plumbing and electrical rework.

## Outcome
Final cost INR 16.2 lakh including GST. Programme: 8 weeks. Bengaluru labour rates ran about 8 percent above national average.
