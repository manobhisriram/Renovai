# Warm minimalist living room and master bedroom, Pune (P-1004)
Client brief: warm, uncluttered rooms with oak tones, cove lighting and a floor-to-ceiling wardrobe. Floor area 30 sqm combined.

## Scope delivered
Gypsum false ceiling with cove lighting, engineered oak flooring, acrylic hinged wardrobe, veneer TV unit, premium emulsion and new LED lighting.

## Outcome
Final cost INR 6.8 lakh including GST. Programme: 6 weeks. Client liked the layered lighting and asked for smart switches later.
