# Compact bathroom refit, apartment, Mumbai (P-1003)
Client brief: modernise a 4 sqm bathroom, fix recurring damp, wall-hung WC and rain shower.

## Scope delivered
Demolition to slab, polymer-modified waterproofing with 48-hour flood test, large-format wall tiles, wall-hung sanitaryware, premium CP fittings, laminate vanity and LED mirror lighting.

## Outcome
Final cost INR 4.1 lakh including GST. Programme: 3 weeks. Mumbai rates ran about 20 percent above national average. Waterproofing flood-test is non-negotiable and was budgeted as its own line.
