# Quotation and approval policy
Quotes are estimates until an on-site survey confirms measurements and hidden conditions. Quotes are valid for 30 days. Every AI-assisted quote is reviewed by a person before it is sent. Quotes above the approval threshold, or with low confidence, need a senior estimator.

## Payment milestones (sample)
20 percent on order confirmation, 40 percent on material delivery, 30 percent on completion of carpentry, 10 percent on handover.

## Warranty (sample)
12 months workmanship warranty. Manufacturer warranties apply to fittings and appliances.
